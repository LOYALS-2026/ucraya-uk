**Website brief**

The site should make it easy to understand what Ucraya offers, choose between individual and group lessons, and enquire. The existing London school provides useful background, while the homepage should focus on learning online.

- Offer online English lessons with UK-based tutors, available one-to-one or in small groups.
- Write for people who want to improve their English. Specific communities, ages and learning priorities remain to be confirmed.
- Use clear, friendly English that learners can understand without specialist vocabulary.
- Use “Enquire about lessons” as the main button wording until prices, booking arrangements and any trial offer are confirmed.
- Explain the connection with the London school and distinguish online lessons from classroom lessons.

No colour palette, logo files, fonts, photography, technology choice or existing code project has been supplied. Do not assume branding from unrelated businesses applies to Ucraya.

**Writing requirements**

Use British English and ordinary, calm language. Keep related thoughts together and let sentences run to their natural stopping point. Avoid hype, artificial urgency, generic introductions, exaggerated claims and repeated short statements followed by explanations. Explain unfamiliar technical terms when they first appear.

Keep public wording focused on the lessons and the learner. Any criticism should concern website performance, never the owner.
