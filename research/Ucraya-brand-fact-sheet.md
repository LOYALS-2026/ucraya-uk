**About paragraph — 120 words**

Ucraya offers online English lessons with UK-based tutors, with a choice of one-to-one tuition or small group classes. It is connected to Training Centre Raya London, whose existing website is ucraya.co.uk. The London school offers General English courses and exam preparation, with classroom teaching at Haverstock School in London. Its existing online provision includes live group courses and individual lessons, and the website lists English courses from elementary to upper-intermediate level. Bulgarian-speaking teachers are available through the existing private lesson service. Learners can contact the school to discuss their English level, learning needs and lesson options. The new Ucraya website focuses on online English lessons, while the existing website provides information about the London school and its range of courses.

Sources: supplied handover for the new brand, formats and UK-based tutors; [About](https://ucraya.co.uk/en/about-us/), [online courses](https://ucraya.co.uk/en/english-online-courses-in-london/) and [private lessons](https://ucraya.co.uk/en/online-courses/) for the existing school.
