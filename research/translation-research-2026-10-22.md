# Ucraya translation service: buyers, languages and content plan

Prepared 22 October 2026 from lili-facts.md, FACTS.md (sections 1 to 3 and 11) and topical-map.csv, plus web research read on 22 October 2026. British English throughout. "Estimate" marks anything not measured.

**What this covers, and what it does not.**
- No keyword volume tool was used. Search phrases are written from the wording seen in search results and forums, and the value rankings are estimates.
- ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity were not queried (section 3 says what was checked instead).
- Web search results come from a US-based search tool, not UK Google, so rankings are indicative only.
- Per Kris's instruction, only English and Bulgarian is treated as a pair the partner offers. Every other pair is treated as not offered.

## 1. Audience truth for translation

The service can only take Bulgarian to English and English to Bulgarian work, so each buyer group is ranked by how much of its translation demand involves Bulgarian.

| Rank | Buyer | Why ranked here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bulgarians in the UK registering UK births and marriages in Bulgaria | Legal duty with a six month deadline. Needs an apostille plus a Bulgarian translation, which matches the partner's core skill (sworn translation and legalisation for Bulgaria). UK agencies rarely write about this direction. |
| 2 | People with Bulgarian documents for a UK application (visa, EUSS family members, citizenship, partner route) | Clear Home Office rule. Large Bulgarian population. Competes with big UK agencies on price and speed. |
| 3 | Bulgarian students applying to UK universities | A diploma and transcript translation leads straight into an IELTS for UKVI sale (£320 for exam preparation). |
| 4 | Bulgarian-trained nurses, doctors and HCPC professions | Small numbers but high value, with strict document rules. Cross-sells OET lessons. |
| 5 | Courts and family matters | Real need, but the work is sensitive, solicitor-led and depends on deadlines. Best reached through solicitor partners. |
| 6 | Businesses trading between the UK and Bulgaria (Service B) | Contracts, register extracts and websites. Steady work that needs B2B outreach. |
| 7 | Employers and right to work checks | Low. Right to work checks use share codes and passports. Translation comes up only for qualification checks. |
| 8 | DVLA licence exchange | Low. A Bulgarian licence is an EU licence, and driving licences are TO CONFIRM in FACTS.md, so do not target this group. |

### 1a. UK visa and settlement applicants (Bulgarian documents)

- **Trigger:** a visa, extension, settlement, EUSS family permit or citizenship application that includes a birth, marriage or divorce document, or a court decision, issued in Bulgarian.
- **The exact rule:**
  - The family route page asks for "a certified translation of any document that is not in English or Welsh" ([gov.uk family visa: information and evidence](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/provide-information), read 22 Oct 2026).
  - The [Skilled Worker caseworker guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/skilled-worker-visa-caseworker-guidance/skilled-worker-caseworker-guidance-accessible) lists five things a translation must have:
    - it comes from a professional translator or company the Home Office can verify independently
    - confirmation that it is an accurate translation
    - the date of the translation
    - the full name and signature of the translator or a company official
    - their contact details
  - Without a valid translation the document is not accepted. This was read through the search summary on 22 Oct 2026, so re-check the page itself before quoting it on the site.
  - The same certified translation rule appears on the [general how to apply page](https://www.gov.uk/apply-to-come-to-the-uk) and on the route pages for Global Talent, Youth Mobility and Seasonal Worker.
- **Documents:** birth certificates, marriage certificates, divorce decisions, civil status certificates, court decisions, police certificates (police certificates are TO CONFIRM in FACTS.md).
- **Search phrases (English):**
  - certified translation bulgarian to english uk
  - bulgarian birth certificate translation home office
  - translate bulgarian marriage certificate for spouse visa
  - ukvi translation requirements
  - who can translate documents for uk visa
- **Search phrases (Bulgarian):**
  - превод на акт за раждане на английски
  - заверен превод за Англия
  - превод на документи за виза Англия
- **Where they ask:**
  - [bg-mamma.com](https://www.bg-mamma.com/?topic=5052) (UK threads)
  - Bulgarian Facebook groups in the UK. Search "Българи в Лондон" and "Българи в Англия" in Facebook. Group names and sizes are not verified.
  - [expat.com Bulgaria forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/europe/bulgaria/1050394-question-about-apostille.html)
  - Reddit r/ukvisa. The search tool returned no threads, so this is not verified.
- **Objections:**
  - Will it be accepted?
  - Can my friend translate it? (No, under the guidance.)
  - Is a scan enough?
  - Do I send the original?
  - How fast is it?

### 1b. University applicants (UK ENIC and admissions)

- **Trigger:** an offer, a CAS deadline, or a UK ENIC statement of comparability.
- **Rule:**
  - Each university sets its own rule. University pages repeat the Home Office list: [Cardiff Met](https://cardiffmet.ac.uk/study/adviceforapplicants/admissions_policy_2023/Pages/Translating-certificates-or-transcripts.aspx), [Liverpool](https://liverpool.ac.uk/studentsupport/visas-and-immigration/student-visa/translations), [Westminster](https://www.westminster.ac.uk/international/visas-and-advice/visas/student-visa/documents-required-for-a-student-visa/translation-guidelines), [BCU](https://www.bcu.ac.uk/international/your-application/translated-documents).
  - [UK ENIC](https://www.enic.org.uk/individuals/statement-of-comparability) has separate "Documents you will need" and "Translation Waiver Service" pages. Those pages could not be read, so its exact translation rule is unverified.
- **Documents:** diplomas, diploma supplements, transcripts.
- **Search phrases:**
  - translate bulgarian diploma for uk university
  - enic translation requirements
  - превод на диплома за университет в Англия
- **Where they ask:** Bulgarian student groups and agents (see section 6), and the university's own offer holder groups.
- **Objections:**
  - Do I translate the supplement too?
  - Is UK ENIC needed?
  - Will it arrive before the CAS deadline?

### 1c. Nurses, doctors and HCPC professions

- **NMC:** the translation must be by "a legally licensed and authorised translator", with written confirmation that it is an exact and direct translation. It must show the translator's full name, address, signature, contact details and stamp. A certified copy of the original-language document goes with it. "We do not accept photocopies of these documents." Source: [NMC supplying documents](https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/supplying-documents/), read 22 Oct 2026.
- **GMC:** a complete translation together with the original-language document. A copy of the original is stamped and signed by the translation service, and the contact details of the translation service are shown. The hard copy is posted to the GMC. Source: [GMC translating documents](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/registration-applications/application-guides/full-registration-for-doctors-who-hold-a-relevant-european-qualification/translating-documents-that-are-not-in-english).
- **HCPC:** "a certified copy of an official translation" for documents not in English. Source: [HCPC international applications guidance](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/online-international-applications-guidance).
- **Partner fit:** the NMC and GMC wording ("licensed and authorised", the stamp) has to be checked against what the partner actually issues before any page says she meets it. This is TO CONFIRM.
- **Market size:** NMC international joiners fell sharply in 2025-26. India had 4,215 joiners, down 54.4%. Nigeria and Ghana were next. Bulgaria is not a top source ([NMC data reports](https://www.nmc.org.uk/globalassets/sitedocuments/data-reports/september-2025/data-report-uk-web.pdf)). Bulgarian nurse volume is small (estimate), but each case is worth a translation plus several OET lessons.
- **Search phrases:**
  - nmc translation requirements
  - bulgarian nurse uk registration
  - gmc translation of documents
  - превод на диплома медицинска сестра Англия

### 1f. Courts and family matters

- **Rule:** under Family Procedure Rules Practice Direction 22A paragraph 8.2, a statement in another language is filed in the original language with a translation, and the translator signs a certificate of accuracy. This is quoted in [M & R (Children) [2025] EWFC 299 (B)](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewfc/b/2025/299). Check the current text on justice.gov.uk.
- **Service abroad:** rule 6.47 asks for a translator's statement giving name, address and qualifications ([legislation.gov.uk FPR 6.47](https://legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2955/rule/6.47/2013-06-24?view=plain)).
- **Documents:** court decisions, divorce papers, civil status certificates, witness statements.
- **Search phrases:**
  - family court translation bulgarian
  - translate court documents uk
  - превод на съдебно решение на английски
- **Best route:** solicitors (section 6).

### 1g. Bulgarians in the UK needing documents for Bulgaria (the strongest fit)

- **Birth and marriage abroad:** the Bulgarian Ministry of Foreign Affairs says Bulgarian citizens abroad must report the event within six months ("в шестмесечен срок"). They can hand the document in at the embassy or directly at their Bulgarian municipality. Sources: [MFA civil status, Bulgarian](https://mfa.bg/bg/uslugi-patuvania/konsulski-uslugi/zad-granica/grajdansko-sastoianie) and [MFA civil status, English](https://mfa.bg/en/services-travel/consular-services/consular-services-abroad/civil-status), read 22 Oct 2026.
- **Requirements:**
  - A certificate from a Hague Convention state needs an apostille.
  - The Bulgarian translation comes from a consular officer or a translation company, with the translator's signature notarised in Bulgaria.
  - Parents then apply for the child's passport once the municipality issues the Bulgarian birth certificate with an ЕГН.
- **UK apostille** ([gov.uk get a document legalised](https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised), read 22 Oct 2026):
  - £45 paper, usually up to 25 working days plus postage
  - £35 e-Apostille, up to 2 working days
  - £40 next-day and £100 same-day, registered businesses only
  - A 25 working day apostille against a six month deadline is the urgency hook.
- **Other triggers:**
  - powers of attorney (property sales and inheritance in Bulgaria)
  - medical documents for TELK or NELK assessments
  - documents for the NSSI (НОИ) or the NRA (НАП)
  - degree certificates for work in Bulgaria
  - Lili's legalisation form lists NRA, NSSI, municipality, court, university and employer as destinations.
- **Documents:** UK birth and marriage certificates, powers of attorney, medical reports, court decisions, degrees.
- **Search phrases (Bulgarian, the main channel):**
  - регистрация на дете родено в Англия
  - апостил на акт за раждане от Англия
  - вписване на брак сключен в Англия
  - пълномощно от Англия за България
  - превод и легализация на документи от Англия
  - медицински документи от Англия за ТЕЛК
- **Search phrases (English):**
  - register uk born baby in bulgaria
  - apostille uk birth certificate for bulgaria
- **Where they ask:**
  - bg-mamma.com
  - Bulgarian Facebook groups in the UK
  - [BG BEN](https://bgben.co.uk), the Bulgarian newspaper in the UK
  - Bulgarian Sunday schools and parent groups (not verified by name)
- **Objections:**
  - Embassy or municipality?
  - Apostille first or translation first?
  - How long does the FCDO take?
  - Can it all be done from the UK?

## 2. Which nationalities and languages to target

The rule applied, per Kris's note of 22 October 2026: lili-facts.md confirms only English and Bulgarian. Every other pair is unconfirmed, so for this plan it is treated as **not offered**, and those groups are recommended for **English lessons only**.

Population figures are from the [ONS Census 2021 language bulletin](https://ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/culturalidentity/language/bulletins/languageenglandandwales/census2021) (released 29 Nov 2022), counting main language, England and Wales:
- Polish 612,000
- Romanian 472,000
- Panjabi 291,000
- Urdu 270,000
- 880,000 people whose main language is not English could not speak English well, and 161,000 could not speak it at all.

The bulletin text read did not give figures for Bulgarian, Bengali, Tamil, Malayalam, Tagalog, Cantonese, Mandarin or Hindi.

- Romanian and Polish community Facebook groups and parish or community centres.
- Hong Kong community organisations in UK cities, for example welcome groups for Hongkongers.
- Nurses' OET Facebook and Telegram groups.
- South Asian community and mosque or gurdwara notice boards for partner visa English.
- Names and sizes were not verified for this report. Check each one before posting.

**What changes if more pairs are confirmed later:**
- Urdu, Bengali and Romanian would be the first to add, because those communities hold civil documents in their own language.
- Hindi, Malayalam, Tagalog and Hong Kong would stay low priority for translation, because their documents are mostly in English or are bilingual already.
