# Ucraya

> Online English lessons with human tutors, offered individually and in small groups.

Draft for ucraya.uk. Facts checked on 21 October 2026. The new site's live pages have not been verified.

## Business facts

- Ucraya is the supplied brand for the new online service; the existing school presents itself as Training Centre Raya London.
- The supplied brief specifies UK-based tutors. This does not establish nationality or native-speaker status.
- The existing school publishes Haverstock School, 24 Haverstock Hill, London NW3 2BQ as its location.
- Its About page states a 2015 founding date. Other age claims conflict and need reconciliation.
- Existing provision includes live online groups, private lessons and classroom courses.
- Published English group levels are A1 to B2, elementary to upper-intermediate.
- The existing school advertises General English, English through English and exam preparation, including IELTS.
- Existing private lessons use Discord, a voice and video platform; the new site's platform is unconfirmed.
- Bulgarian-speaking tutors are advertised for existing private lessons. Other support languages are unconfirmed.
- Existing prices include £15 per 55-minute private lesson and £298 per online group course. The fees table calls private lessons hourly; confirm the charging basis.
- New-site prices, group limits and a live trial are unconfirmed. The published free lesson offer is an emailed video.
- Existing contact: office@ucraya.co.uk; +44 7459 702621.
