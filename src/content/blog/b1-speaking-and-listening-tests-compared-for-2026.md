Both are Secure English Language Tests (SELT) on the gov.uk list. Trinity's Graded Examinations in Spoken English (GESE) Grade 5 is a 10 minute one-to-one conversation. IELTS (International English Language Testing System) Life Skills B1 takes around 20 minutes with one other test taker. Compare format and fees, and check your route on gov.uk first.

## What every B1 test has in common

B1 is a level on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). The gov.uk [SELT guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt) says the level you need depends on your immigration route, so read the guidance for your own route first. We read that page on 21 November 2026.

The same page sets three rules for a result to be accepted. The test must be on the approved list, it must have been sat at an approved test location, and it must have been awarded in the two years before your application. It also warns that only the tests and levels named on the list are approved, so check the exact test name when you book.

Every test in this guide is a speaking and listening test, taken in person. You are being judged on whether you can follow and take part in everyday conversation, not on perfect grammar.

One change to watch. A banner on the Trinity page says that from 26 March 2027, most people applying for settlement, also called Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) will need a B2 speaking and listening test, with some routes still needing B1. We did not see that change on the gov.uk page above, so check gov.uk for your route and your application date before you book any B1 test.

## Trinity GESE Grade 5: format and timing

The [Trinity B1 page](https://www.trinitycollege.com/qualifications/SELT/B1-GESE-grade-5) describes GESE Grade 5 as a 10 minute speaking and listening test with an examiner, one to one. It has two parts. In the topic part you choose a topic in advance, complete a Topic Form and bring it to the exam for discussion. In the conversation part you talk with the examiner about two subject areas the examiner chooses.

The page says the test can be taken at a Trinity centre, and that results are emailed provisionally by 9am the day after the exam, with a link to the digital certificate within 4 to 7 days. It also says Trinity recommends that, for immigration purposes, only certificates less than two years old are accepted.

## IELTS Life Skills B1: format and timing

IELTS describes its Life Skills test as a speaking and listening test. On the [format page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-tests-for-uk-visas-and-immigration/ielts-life-skills-test/life-skills-test-format-in-detail), you take the B1 test with an examiner and one other test taker, and the test takes around 20 minutes. Tasks cover everyday subjects such as work, health, transport and housing. At B1 they may include narrating, asking about past or future events and showing contrast or cause.

The [IELTS Life Skills page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-tests-for-uk-visas-and-immigration/ielts-life-skills-test) says the test is not scored. You receive a Pass or a Fail, and only a Pass can be used in your application. For waiting times and fees, check the IELTS page or test centre for the location you plan to use.

## Other approved providers to look at

The gov.uk page lists four providers for people in the UK: Trinity College London, the IELTS SELT Consortium, LANGUAGECERT and Pearson. It names LANGUAGECERT's tests as International ESOL SELT, Academic SELT and General SELT, and Pearson's as PTE Academic UKVI (UK Visas and Immigration) and PTE Home. Pearson Test of English (PTE) Home is the one that matters here.

The [Pearson PTE Home B1 page](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-home/pte-home-b1) describes a computer-based speaking and listening test of about 22 minutes, with results typically in 2 days. The price depends on where you take the test, according to the page. Its page also mentions 30 minutes at one point, so check the current length on the day you book.

For LANGUAGECERT, the pages that would show a B1 speaking and listening test did not load for us. Check the provider page, and do not rely on us for its format or length.

## Retake rules and fees to compare

Retake rules differ, and fees change. We give a figure only where we read it on a provider page, on the same line as the link. Where we did not, the line says to check the provider page.

- Trinity: GESE exams cost £160 and Exam Protect, an add-on, costs £35 for one retake and one reschedule, according to the [Trinity page](https://www.trinitycollege.com/qualifications/SELT/B1-GESE-grade-5) we read on 21 November 2026.
- IELTS Life Skills B1: the fee and any retake terms, check the [IELTS Life Skills page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-tests-for-uk-visas-and-immigration/ielts-life-skills-test) and your test centre.
- Pearson PTE Home B1: the price depends on country, so use the price finder on the [Pearson page](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-home/pte-home-b1).
- LANGUAGECERT: check the provider page.

The gov.uk page adds that in certain circumstances, if you failed one component of a test, you may be allowed to sit that component again, and that you must read the provider's terms before booking. For a one-part speaking and listening test, ask the provider what its retake rule is before you pay.
