# Content research prompt for Claude (90 day plan, second pass)

Use in a new claude.ai chat. Model: **Claude Opus 5.5** with extended thinking on and web search on (Research mode if your plan shows it). Effort: high. Expect 20 to 40 minutes. Attach these three files from the ucraya-uk repository before sending: `research/segments.csv`, `content/topical-map.csv` and `content/FACTS.md`. If the chat cannot take attachments, paste the pillar list and the first 20 rows of the map instead.

Copy everything below the line.

---

You are a senior growth and content strategist for a UK online English school. Do real web research (official pages, forums, community groups, competitor sites) and show your sources with the date you read them. British English. No em dashes. Be honest about what you could not verify.

THE BUSINESS (facts, do not change them)
- Brand: Ucraya, website https://ucraya.uk, the online lessons service of Training Centre Raya London, a London English school since 2015 (ucraya.co.uk). Lessons are live on a video call with real human tutors who teach for the London school.
- Products and prices: one-to-one 55 minute lessons £15 (or £20 for lessons taught entirely in English); a 12 week small group level course (A1 to B2, no more than 12 people, on Zoom) £298; exam preparation (IELTS, Cambridge and other certificates) £320 for 24 hours. Payment by bank transfer after the office confirms. One free live group class to try. No tests are run by the school; it prepares people for tests run by others. No pass guarantees, no accreditation claims.
- Site pages that articles can send readers to: /courses/oet/, /courses/selt-b1/, /courses/ielts/, /courses/business-english/, /courses/kids/, /courses/general-english/, /courses/one-to-one/, /courses/group-courses/, /pricing/, /free-trial/, /placement-test/.
- Languages on the site: English, Bulgarian, Romanian, Polish. Traditional Chinese is under consideration.
- Edge over competitors to use: a qualified tutor from a London school at £15 a lesson is roughly half the common UK marketplace rate; the school has 5 star Google reviews and has taught since 2015; lessons are built around a goal and a deadline, not a syllabus.

THE GOAL
Enquiries from people who are ready to pay because they have a deadline: nurses retaking OET or IELTS for NMC registration; settlement and citizenship applicants who need the B1 speaking and listening test (including Hong Kong BN(O) visa holders reaching five years from 2026 onwards); partners on family visas needing A2 or B1; IELTS candidates with a university offer; Skilled Worker applicants needing B2; professionals with a booked interview; parents acting on school feedback; care employers wanting better communication from staff. A first pass of this research was done in ChatGPT (attached: segments.csv and topical-map.csv). Treat it as a draft to improve, not as truth: it had no search volumes, no competitor pricing, missed the Hong Kong settlement timing, and treated the care employer segment as proven.

WHAT TO PRODUCE

Part 1, audience truth. Re-rank the segments with evidence: for each one give the trigger and its timing (official rule and date, with the source), where these people actually ask questions (name the specific forums, Facebook groups, Reddit threads, WhatsApp or Telegram communities, YouTube channels, with links), the exact phrases they use in English and in their first language, what they pay today (real competitor prices with links: Preply, italki, Cambly, British Council, specialist OET and SELT coaches), their top three objections, and a score for intent, size and fit. State plainly which segments you would drop or add, and why. Give a specific view on Hong Kong Cantonese speakers in the UK: size, timing, where they gather, what they search, and whether a Traditional Chinese version of the site is worth it.

Part 2, competitor and gap map. The ten pages that currently rank in UK Google for the twenty most valuable questions across the segments (list the questions, the ranking page, what it does well, what it misses). Then the questions where no good page exists today. Then what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answer today for the ten most valuable questions (quote the answer shape and which sources they cite), so we know what a page must contain to be cited.

Part 3, the 90 day plan as a CSV. Replace or improve the attached topical-map.csv. Rules: EXACTLY these columns in this order: day,publish_date,pillar,cluster,segment,funnel_stage,title,primary_keyword,secondary_keywords,ai_question,search_intent,money_page,cta,outline,original_element,faq,language,word_count,status,url. 90 rows, day 1 to 90, publish_date empty, language en, status planned, url empty, word_count 900 to 1400. 6 to 8 pillars that are buying reasons, each with a pillar guide in the first 9 days. Mix about 40 percent awareness, 35 percent consideration, 25 percent decision. Every row: one money page from the list above; the exact natural language question a person types into ChatGPT or Google (ai_question); 3 to 6 secondary keywords separated by "; "; 5 to 7 H2 headings separated by " | "; an original_element that a generic answer cannot have (a checklist, a worked example labelled as made up, a decision table, a timeline from an official rule, a cost comparison with sources); three FAQ pairs as "Q? :: A." separated by " || "; a title of 59 characters or fewer, UK spelling, specific, no clickbait; titles, keywords and questions unique. Order the first 14 days for the two highest intent segments. Keep the three already published (days 1 to 3 of the attached map) as they are. If you keep a row from the ChatGPT map, keep its wording; if you change it, say so in a separate list. Provide the CSV as a downloadable file and print it in three code blocks (days 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90).

Part 4, distribution per article. For each pillar: the three places the articles should be shared (specific groups, subreddits, forums, with links and their posting rules), the hook sentence to use there, and which article each week should be turned into a 60 second tutor video or a short post. Also the five partnership types worth one email each (care agencies, nursing recruiters, immigration solicitors, community organisations, colleges), with the one line offer.

Part 5, the first 30 days of traffic without search rankings. A week by week plan: what the school's existing site, Google Business Profile and Facebook page should do, a £300 Google Ads test with exact keywords and negative keywords, and the one metric to watch each week.

Keep the writing plain and specific. Where you estimate, say "estimate". Where a rule can change (visa rules, NMC requirements, test fees), give the date you checked it and the official page.
