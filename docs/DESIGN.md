# ucraya.uk design system: liquid glass (light theme)

Source of truth: `src/styles/global.css`. One token block in `:root`, then utility classes, then components. Everything below the tokens sits in cascade layers (`base`, `components`), so Tailwind utilities in templates (`mt-3`, `w-full`, `lg:sticky`) still win over component rules. `src/components/blog/blog.css` is NOT layered, so it beats the layered rules whatever the specificity: keep it to blog-only classes (the old `:where(.btn-secondary)` fallbacks that turned the pillar page buttons black were removed for this reason).

## Logo (the ORIGINAL Raya London design, supplied vectors)
- Rule: the original artwork only. The owner supplied it as true vectors in `public/raya/original/`: `raya-original-vector-black.svg` (light backgrounds), `raya-original-vector-white.svg` (dark backgrounds), `raya-original-vector-navy.svg`, plus 1259 px PNG exports of the black and white versions. Aspect 1259 by 471. The lettering reads TRAINING CENTRE "RAYA" LONDON under four silver rimmed badges (eye, ear, pencil, lips). Never recolour, re-letter, rearrange, crop, distort or add glass to it, and never draw a small imitation of it (a four circle wordmark in a hero, for example). The handover notes are in `research/brand/raya-original-vector-pack/CLAUDE-CODE-HANDOVER.md`.
- The full SVGs are 234 KB each, too heavy for a bar, so pages use optimised full colour PNGs with a transparent background made by `npm run brand` (`scripts/gen-brand.mjs`, sharp: rendered from the vector at four times the size and reduced with lanczos, no palette quantising): `public/brand/raya-black-300.png`, `-400.png`, `-600.png` (15, 22 and 39 KB) and `raya-white-480.png`, `-720.png` (29 and 50 KB). The check:ui suite fails if one is 60 KB or more. The SVGs stay at their own path for print and for anyone who needs them.
- Header: `raya-black-400.png` with `srcset` 300w, 400w and 600w and `sizes="(min-width: 768px) 200px, 150px"`, inside a link to the locale home with `aria-label` "Training Centre Raya London, home" (`header.homeLabel`, four languages) and an empty `alt` so the name is spoken once. 150 px wide on phones (132 px under 360 px), 200 px from 768 px. The bar is 76 px tall on phones and 96 px from 768 px (`--bar-h` 4.75rem and 6rem). The desktop menu starts at 1280 px (it was 1180 px): the content column is 72rem, and the 200 px logo, nine items (Translation was added) and the Bulgarian labels need that room; nav links are 0.92 rem, never wrap, and the language button and Enquire are slightly tighter. Under 1280 px the menu button is shown.
- Footer: `raya-white-480.png` (srcset with 720w) at 240 px straight on the near black footer, lazy loaded, above the line "Ucraya is the online lessons service of Training Centre Raya London" (`footer.service`). The cream tile of the earlier pack is gone.
- Favicons: `scripts/gen-brand.mjs` cuts the four `<g id="badge-N">` groups (disc, silver rim and symbol, with the original gradient defs) out of the original SVG and places them two by two, changing only their position. Output: `public/favicon.ico` (32 and 48 px PNG images inside an ICO), `public/brand/favicon-32.png` and `favicon-48.png` (transparent), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` (on the cream page colour, because a transparent icon turns black on some phones). No SVG favicon: four badges with their gradients are over 200 KB as an SVG.
- Open Graph image (`public/og/default.png`, 1200 x 630): the original white lettering logo, 840 px wide, on the dark gradient with the strap line and "ucraya.uk" (text outlined with opentype.js).
- Structured data: Organization and EducationalOrganization are named "Training Centre Raya London", alternate names "Ucraya" and "Raya London", `logo` is `https://ucraya.uk/raya/original/raya-original-vector-black-1259.png`, `sameAs` Facebook, Instagram and the Google reviews link.
- The earlier redesigned pack (twelve SVGs and `raya-flat-light.png` in `public/raya/`) and the old `public/brand/logo.svg` stay in the repo for the owner but nothing references them; the site checker fails if a page links to `/brand/logo.svg`.

## Tokens (`:root`)
- Brand: amber `#ffb606` (primary), green `#4caf50` (secondary), orange `#f24c0a`, navy `#1f2a44`, ink `#1a1a1a`, body `#444444`, muted `#5f5f5f` (darker than the brand sheet's `#6b6b6b` so it keeps AA over the blobs). Logo colours as bare RGB triples for the background only: `--logo-cyan` 0 173 220 (#00ADDC), `--logo-lime` 157 204 54 (#9DCC36), `--logo-yellow` 235 195 0 (#EBC300), `--logo-red` 228 0 53 (#E40035).
- Surfaces: `--surface` white .62, `--surface-strong` .82, `--surface-reading` .92, `--bar-fill` cream .86.
- Shape: `--r-md` 16px, `--r-lg` 24px. Blur `--blur` 18px, `--blur-lg` 24px, saturation 140%. Rim `--rim` white .72, `--edge` hairline, `--sheen` and `--sheen-lens` (stronger top left highlight, for hero, price and quote cards), `--hairline`, `--shadow-1`, `--shadow-2`, `--glow-amber`, `--glow-green`. Motion 160 and 240 ms, sweep 220 ms.
- Type: Roboto Slab headings, PT Sans body, no new fonts. h1 40 to 56 px (30 px under 360 px), h2 28 to 36, body 17/18 px at 1.6. Section padding 72 to 120 px.
- Fonts are self hosted and declared in `global.css` with `unicode-range` per script (latin, latin-ext, cyrillic), so an English page downloads three woff2 files (about 107 KB) and a Bulgarian page adds the Cyrillic ones. The fontsource per-subset CSS had no `unicode-range` and made every page load all nine files (240 KB). Roboto Slab 600 is not used and not loaded.

## Background (mesh)
Four fixed radial blobs in `.mesh` (Base.astro), very pale versions of the logo colours so the page and the logo read as one family: yellow top left (.26), lime right (.17), cyan bottom left (.12), red bottom right (.055). Slow drift on transform only; `will-change` on the four blobs; stopped under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Contrast measured against the palest and warmest areas: ink above 12:1, body above 7:1, muted above 5:1.

## Classes (keep these names)
`.glass` (.62 fill, rim, top highlight, 24px, soft shadow), `.glass-strong` (.82, text heavy cards), `.glass-bar` (sticky header and phone bar), `.btn` with `.btn-primary` (amber), `.btn-secondary` (glass), `.btn-whatsapp` (green; old names `.btn-outline`, `.btn-green`, `.btn-light` still work), `.card` (glass-strong plus padding), `.pill`, `.input` (16px font, 44px tall). Also: `.card-accent` with `.accent-amber|green|orange|navy`, `.glow-amber`, `.glow-green`, `.price-card` and `.quote-card` (lens highlight), `.prose-lite` (reading surface .92), `.hero-panel`, `.cta-panel`, `.quote-strip` with `.dots`, `.tutor-row`, `.estimator`, `.guides-strip`, `.dock`, `.dock-cta`, `.to-top`, `.lesson`.

## Where glass is, and is not
- Glass fill: header bar, phone bottom bar, cards, hero panel, pricing cards, testimonials, FAQ items, modal, dropdowns, the floating dock, the search popover, the hero level chips, quiz cards.
- Backdrop blur only where something floats over content: `.glass` that is not a `.card`, `.hero-panel`, `.modal-card`, `.lang-menu`, `.nav-panel`, `.cookie`, `.btn-secondary`, the bar (on its `::before`), the back to top button, the dock, the search popover and the hero chips. Cards in lists, FAQ items, steps, quiz fieldsets and form cards are not blurred (at .82 to .92 fill the blur is invisible and it costs scrolling speed on mid range Android).
- Not glass: long reading text (`.prose-lite`, legal, article bodies, FAQ answers). The article body sits on the mesh (the .92 surface is used by `.prose-lite` on money pages only); contrast on the warmest area is about 8:1. Amber is never used as text colour.
- Fallbacks: `@supports not (backdrop-filter)`, `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` and `prefers-contrast: more` switch the surface tokens to near opaque and drop the blur (contrast mode also uses ink borders). `prefers-reduced-motion` stops the drift and all transitions and animations.
- The top hairline of a glass surface is its inset box-shadow (a gradient layer with a 1 px size broke the sheen layer on cards that carry two tint gradients).

## Interactions (all optional polish; nothing needs JavaScript to work)
- Cards and buttons: hover lift (cards 3 px, buttons 2 px), a specular sweep (a soft band of light crosses the element in 220 ms, `::before`, transform and opacity only, hover devices only, `overflow: hidden` on `.btn`, `.card` and `.hero-panel` only; never on elements that hold a pop-up list), pressed state (scale .98), focus rings (3 px navy). Under reduced motion transitions are off and the band stays out of sight.
- Header (`public/js/nav.js`): on phones it hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up; never while the menu is open or focus is inside it, within 120 px of the top, or for reduced motion. From 768 px an Enquire button sits in the header.
- Phone menu: the button is icon only under 480 px (label kept for screen readers), turns into a cross when open, and the bottom bar is hidden while the menu is open.
- Back to top (`public/js/ui.js`): a glass button after 800 px, keyboard reachable only while visible. From 768 px it sits in the dock row (see below).
- FAQ: details/summary with a smooth height change (Web Animations API on the details element; skipped for reduced motion; the native element still does the work).
- Testimonials on the home page: one row of four compact cards (the Google rating card first); on phones a swipeable strip with CSS scroll snap and dots under it (`data-strip` and `data-dots`, built by `ui.js`); a grid from 900 px.
- Placement test: the sticky progress strip is a small reading-surface panel; a chosen answer gives a short pop; the result reads "Your result" and "Level A2".
- Pricing page: "What will it cost me?" estimator (`public/js/estimator.js`): one-to-one at 15 or 20 pounds (read from the page's own prices), one to five lessons a week, 4.33 weeks a month, "a guide only; you pay per lesson by bank transfer". The default (one lesson a week at 15 pounds) is rendered into the HTML.

## Floating dock (768 px and up)
The strip with "Contact", Enquire and WhatsApp on the footer edge was removed in both looks (it had no breathing space and repeated the phone bar). Its job on desktop and tablet is the dock: `Dock.astro` (in `Base.astro`, after the footer) holds `.dock-cta` (a glass pill with `Enquire` through `EnquireLink`, carrying the page's goal, and `WhatsApp`, two 44 px buttons with the specular sweep) and the back to top button, in one fixed row 24 px from the bottom and right edges. `ui.js` adds `.is-on` to `.dock-cta` after about 600 px of scrolling (220 ms fade and rise, transform and opacity only; under reduced motion opacity only) and removes it while the footer comes within 80 px (IntersectionObserver), while any search list is open (`html.search-open`, set by `search.js`) and while the pop-in is open (`html.modal-open`; the dialog is in the top layer anyway). `--dock-lift` raises the row above the slim article bar while that is showing. The back to top button stays in the row when the pill steps aside. Under 768 px the dock is `display: contents`, the pill is not drawn and the bottom bar does the job; back to top keeps its own place above the bar.

## Search popover
The suggestion list (`.bl-suggest`, inside `.bl-search-field`) is a floating glass panel under the input: as wide as the input plus the button, 16 px radius, `.glass-strong` fill with blur, 1 px rim, soft shadow, `overflow-x: hidden` and `overflow-y: auto` with a thin scrollbar, at most 60% of the screen height and no taller than the room below the field (`search.js` sets `max-height`; it opens upwards, `.is-up`, only when there is under about 220 px below and more room above). Rows are one line: the title with an ellipsis and a small pillar pill at the right. Escape, a press outside and choosing a row close it. Nothing around it may clip it: `.bl-side` (the sticky article sidebar) keeps `overflow: visible`, and only its contents list scrolls when it is long. Never give a wrapper of a search box `overflow: auto` or `hidden`, and do not set a background in an unlayered rule on `.bl-suggest` other than through the glass tokens (unlayered rules beat the layered glass fill). The same box is used on the article sidebar and end, the hub, the pillar pages and the questions page.

## Hero motion (home page)
`HeroArt.astro` is a still illustration with HTML overlays; `public/js/hero.js` (home page only, no library) makes it a mock lesson. Rules: transform and opacity only, no layout reads in the animation (the caption is typed with `requestAnimationFrame` by time), the loop stops when the hero leaves the screen (IntersectionObserver) or the tab is hidden, and after about 40 s of visible time it settles into a calm state (`.is-calm`). Without JavaScript or with reduced motion nothing moves and the caption shows the first line. Parts: LIVE pill (`.live-pill`, pulsing dot), speaker ring and three audio bars that follow `data-speaker` (tutor or student, one change per caption line), caption strip (`.lesson-cap`: label, then the line typed over about 2 s and held about 1.6 s), a drawn SVG tick after the fourth line (`.lesson-react`), three glass chips (`.lesson-chip`, 6 to 8 s float), and a tilt of at most 4 degrees towards a mouse (200 ms ease, none on touch or reduced motion). The lines live in `home.live` in each language file: the Tutor lines and labels are translated, the Student lines stay English because the lesson is in English. The headline is `home.h1Lines` (two lines, a fixed break so no word is split; `h1` has `hyphens: none` and `text-wrap: balance`); words rise in 40 ms apart, then the lead 20 ms apart, then the buttons, in under 900 ms (CSS only, delays by `nth-of-type`, no inline styles). The hero art shows on phones too (22 rem wide).

## Language dropdown
`details.lang` in the header (flag SVG plus code), menu of four flags and native names (system font, so the Cyrillic and Latin Extended subsets are not downloaded for the names) with the current one ticked; each links to the same page in that locale. Flags are drawn SVGs in `public/brand/flags/`. `public/js/lang.js` adds Escape, arrow keys and outside click; without JavaScript details/summary still works. On phones the menu panel also lists the four languages.

## Pop-in enquiry contract
- Every Enquire button is `<EnquireLink locale goal format level purpose>`. It renders `<a data-enquire data-goal data-format data-level data-purpose href="/contact/?...#enquiry">`: JavaScript opens the pop-in prefilled, without JavaScript the link goes to the contact page (one query choice only, because an `&` in a static href fails the site checker). Raw `<a data-enquire data-goal="oet" href="...">` works too (blog CTA boxes).
- Article pages pass the article's goal, level and purpose to `Base.astro` (`enquire` prop), so the header and the phone bar's Enquire buttons open the pop-in prefilled from the first paint.
- On `/contact/` and `/free-trial/` the inline form is the page, so Enquire scrolls to `#enquiry` instead (`inline` prop, set by `Base.astro`).
- `EnquiryModal.astro` is in `Base.astro`, so every page has the form in static HTML (Netlify detection) inside a native `<dialog id="enquiry-modal">`. The inline copy and the pop-in are the same Netlify form `enquiry`, same field names (`form-fit.mjs` checks this).
- Fields: email (first, for Reply-To), name, phone (optional), goal chips (single choice), format chips, "When is your test or deadline?" (an optional drop-down, `deadline_pick`, copied into the hidden `deadline`), message (one line on phones), consent tick, hidden fields (level, purpose, first-language, deadline, preferred-times, detail, enquiry_page, site_language, source fields). Honeypot `bot-field`.
- Fit: every control is on screen at 375 x 667 with no scrolling in all four locales, and at 1280 x 720 (`@media (max-width: 639px) and (max-height: 700px)` compacts chips, inputs (40 px) and the head). Bottom sheet under 640 px, centred card above.
- Behaviour: scroll lock (`html.modal-open`), native focus trap and inert background, Escape and the X close, click on the dimmed area closes, focus returns to the opener. On a fine pointer the email field is focused; on a touch screen the sheet takes focus so the keyboard does not cover the form. Each opening starts clean: a goal, format or level left by an earlier opening is cleared unless the visitor chose it themselves.
- Mobile: inputs are 16px or more (global rule), so iPhone never zooms; the viewport meta still allows zoom. `autocomplete` name, email, tel; `inputmode`, `spellcheck=false`, `enterkeyhint`.
- Validation: friendly messages on blur from `form.errors.*`. The send button is never disabled: pressing it with a problem shows every message and focuses the first field that needs attention. Loading state on submit. The POST is a normal form post to the locale's thank-you page; success is never shown without it.

## Where an enquiry came from (`public/js/attribution.js`)
Hidden fields filled from the first touch of a visit, kept in localStorage key `ucraya-attribution` for 30 days: `source_channel` (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, Google search, Bing search, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Email or WhatsApp, Direct or unknown), `source_referrer` (200 chars), `landing_page`, `utm_source|medium|campaign|content`, `visit_started`. A `utm_source` of chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com or openai is recorded as ChatGPT, perplexity as Perplexity, gemini as Gemini, copilot as Copilot, claude as Claude (so one channel has one name whether the visitor came with a referrer or with ChatGPT's `?utm_source=chatgpt.com`); any other `utm_source` is kept as written. `last_channel` and `last_referrer` record how THIS browser session began (sessionStorage), so a visitor who first came direct and returns from ChatGPT shows both. Set on every open or submit: `enquiry_page`, `device` (phone, tablet, desktop), `browser_language`, `placement_level`. The `subject` becomes "New enquiry (<goal>) via <channel>: ucraya.uk". Disclosed in the privacy page. First party, no advertising cookies. If the owner's adviser treats the localStorage record as needing consent under PECR, switch the key off in one place (`firstTouch`).

## Translation pages and the quote form
- Pages: `/translation/` (hub), `/translation/certified/`, `/translation/specialist/` and the noindex `/translation/thank-you/`, in all four locales (`Translation.astro`, `TranslationService.astro`, `TranslationThanks.astro`; copy under `translation` in the four JSON files; Bulgarian first in importance, all three non English files are machine written and need a native read). The service pages have the same shape as a course page: hero, reading column with a sticky price card ("Free quote for every job", never a price), FAQ, the form, related cards.
- Nav: "Translation" sits after Courses in the header and first in the footer's Ucraya column. Short labels in every language (Translation, Преводи, Traduceri, Tłumaczenia). On the translation pages the header and phone bar Enquire buttons scroll to the quote form (`inline`, wrapper id `enquiry`) instead of opening the lessons pop-in. The home page has a strip "Also from the school: document translation" under "Who our lessons are for" (`.tq-strip`, `.strip-card`), the pricing page a section "Document translation: quoted per job", the About page one line.
- Form (`TranslationQuoteForm.astro`, static on the three pages so Netlify detects it): Netlify form `translation-quote`, `data-netlify`, honeypot `bot-field`, `enctype="multipart/form-data"`, action to the locale's `/translation/thank-you/`, hidden `form-name` and `subject` ("Translation quote request via ucraya.uk: %{submissionId}"). Fields: `email` (first), `name`, `phone`, `deadline` (date), `from_language`, `to_language`, `other_language` (shown only when Other is chosen), `document_type`, `purpose`, `certification` (preselected Certified translation on the certified page, else Not sure), `pages`, `message`, `file_1`, `file_2`, `file_3` (accept pdf, jpg, jpeg, png, heic, docx; 8 MB in total, Netlify's limit), `consent`, `bot-field`. Hidden: `page_service`, `enquiry_page`, `site_language` and the attribution fields filled by `public/js/attribution.js` (`source_channel`, `source_referrer`, `last_channel`, `last_referrer`, `landing_page`, `utm_*`, `device`, `browser_language`, `visit_started`). Option values sent to Lili are always English; the labels are translated.
- Layout and behaviour: two columns from 640 px, one on phones; 16 px controls at least 44 px tall; the three file inputs stack in one column. `public/js/translation.js` validates on blur and on send (the same rules as the enquiry form: every message shown, first problem focused), refuses more than 8 000 000 bytes in total or a file type outside the list with a message that says what is wrong, toggles the Other box, and shows the sending state. Without JavaScript the browser still posts the form. It is NOT asked to fit one screen (the file fields make that impossible): `form-fit.mjs` checks fonts, columns, no sideways scroll and identical field names in all locales.
- Wording rules (FACTS section 11): no price, no promise of acceptance, no "sworn translator in the UK", "official translator" or "Home Office approved"; "a registered and qualified translator who has worked with the school for years"; the translator's email address is never printed.

## Using the classes on a new page
Wrap page content in `.section` and `.wrap`; a narrow reading column is `.wrap.narrow` (the container keeps the page's left edge, its children are 46 rem wide and left aligned). Start with `<PageHero h1 lead />` (a `.glass.hero-panel`); use `.card`/`.glass-strong` for text blocks, `.prose-lite` for long reading, `.grid-cards` for card grids, `.btn-primary` for the one main action per block, `.btn-secondary` for the rest, `.btn-whatsapp` for WhatsApp. In heroes use two buttons at most; WhatsApp is the text link `a.wa-link.hero-wa` (desktop only; phones have it in the bottom bar). Put Enquire buttons through `EnquireLink`. Links inside body text are underlined (a colour only cue fails WCAG 1.4.1). Never use inline `style` attributes or inline scripts (the Content Security Policy blocks them).

`npm run build && npm run check` (the checker also verifies llms.txt under 350 words, llms-full.txt listing every guide, the robots rules, speakable JSON-LD on money pages and BlogPosting fields), `npm run check:ui` (`scripts/ui-checks.mjs`: suggestions, the search popover on three page types, the dock, the hero motion, phone menu, one bar on a phone article, tablet Enquire, lists and underlines, estimator, header hide, back to top, FAQ, placement, stale prefill, utm names, no sideways scroll at 375 and 320 in four languages), `node scripts/shots.mjs`, `node scripts/form-fit.mjs` (iPhone SE and desktop, four locales: fit, 16px fonts, Escape, matching field names). Compiled CSS is 53 KB for the site plus 13 KB for the blog (keep each page under 70 KB). Lighthouse was not run in the build container.
